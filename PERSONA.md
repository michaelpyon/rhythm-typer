# PERSONA.md - Rhythm Typer

## The person

**Dan Okafor, 25, junior backend dev in Austin.** Remote job, mechanical keyboard on his desk (Keychron Q1 with lubed switches he posted once to r/MechanicalKeyboards). He warms up every morning with 3 Monkeytype runs before standup and his profile says 118 WPM / 97.8% accuracy. He plays osu! casually on weekends and has A Dance of Fire and Ice in his Steam library. He is not looking for typing practice. He is looking for a reason to flex the skill he already ground out.

This keeps the V2 evangelist (22 to 28, r/WebGames + r/typing, Monkeytype baseline) and sharpens it to a named individual. No overrule needed: the V2 read was correct, because this game's only durable hook is a defensible personal best, and that is exactly the Monkeytype-brained player. The one refinement: he is as much a rhythm-game person as a typing person, so the pitch should lead with "typing meets rhythm timing" rather than "typing game with sharks."

## The job he hires it for

A 90 second skill flex between tasks. Not practice (Monkeytype owns that), not a long session game (osu! owns that). The job: "prove I can type fast AND on beat, get a number that says so, beat that number tomorrow." Secondary job: have a link to drop when someone in Discord says "I type 100 WPM," as a gauntlet.

## Where he hangs out online

- r/typing and r/MechanicalKeyboards (lurks, occasionally posts scores)
- r/WebGames (where he would first find this)
- The Monkeytype Discord, #scores channel
- Typing/keyboard X: follows Monkeytype's account, keyboard build creators, and rhythm game clip accounts
- osu! circles adjacent: watches beatmap clip compilations on YouTube

## What he uses today instead

- **Monkeytype** for raw WPM (his identity metric)
- **ZType** when he wants typing-as-a-game (he considers it the genre classic but stale)
- **TypeRacer** occasionally, for head to head
- osu! / ADOFAI for rhythm itch, no typing involved

Rhythm Typer's wedge: nothing he uses scores the intersection of typing speed and beat timing. "% perfect" is a stat none of his current tools can produce.

## Taste: 3 named products he considers premium

1. **Monkeytype** - dark, minimal, monospace, zero clutter, instant restart, everything is a stat. His gold standard for "a web tool that respects me."
2. **osu!** - juice, hitsounds, combo fire, the feeling that PERFECT timing is celebrated loudly.
3. **A Dance of Fire and Ice** - one-input rhythm purity, brutal precision windows, clean geometric visuals.

If Rhythm Typer reads like the intersection of those 3, he trusts it. If it reads like a Flash-era edu-game, he closes the tab.

## His vocabulary

WPM, raw, accuracy, consistency, PB (personal best), combo, FC (full combo), "on beat," timing window, ms windows, "tighten the window," clutch, choke, grind, seed/run. He says "I got a 41% perfect run" not "I scored well." Copy should speak in stats and windows, never in "fun for the whole family" tone.

## His device

MacBook Pro or desktop with an external mechanical keyboard, Chrome, often with headphones already on. Desktop-only gate is not a problem for him, it is a filter he approves of. He will never arrive on mobile except from a tweet, so the mobile view only needs to say "keyboard required" with confidence, not apologize.

## 5-second bounce triggers

1. No visible number to beat (no PB shown on the title screen = "this forgets me")
2. Comic Sans energy: childish art, clip-art sharks, anything that looks like a kids' typing tutor
3. Laggy or mushy input feel on the first 3 keystrokes
4. A tutorial wall before he can press a key (he wants to be typing within 5 seconds)
5. Silent start: a rhythm game with no audible beat immediately reads as broken

## The unprompted-share moment

Game over screen showing a run line that flexes 2 skills at once: **"Score 12,480 | Wave 7 | 34x max combo | 61% PERFECT on Hard (150 BPM)"** with a NEW BEST flare. The screenshot must be self-explanatory to someone who has never seen the game: big score, the % perfect stat, the difficulty and BPM visible. Bonus GIF moment: a full-combo stretch where every hit flashes PERFECT in sync with the beat pips, which is clip-bait for the Monkeytype Discord. The one-click Share on X button carries the enriched stat line; the share text is the product's growth loop.
